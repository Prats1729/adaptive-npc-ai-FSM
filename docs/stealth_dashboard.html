Research Gap Analysis & Scientific Contribution

Research Gap 1: The "Reactive Lag" Flaw in Base Paper

Problem: In Xu & Verbrugge (2025), the Information Field pulls guards toward historical sightings: I(n) = -I0 * γ^d(pt, n). Because the player moves at 120% speed running MaxMin evasion, guards are constantly chasing where the player used to be, allowing evasive players to escape into side passages.
Our Solution (PI-PF): We predict the player's future escape corridor using velocity extrapolation: p_pred = pt + τ * v. On Test Maze, this raised capture rate from 56% to 70% and dropped capture time to 29.2s on Miami.

Research Gap 2: The "Hand-Tuned Heuristic" Flaw in Base Paper

Problem: The base paper relies on static, manually tuned constants (wI=0.9, wC=0.5, wN=0.1) without any data-driven learning.
Our Solution (RL-Q Agent): We implemented a Reinforcement Learning agent using the Bellman Equation: Q(s, a) ← Q(s, a) + α [R + γ max Q(s', a') - Q(s, a)] with state discretization and combat rewards. On Test Maze, RL-Q achieved the highest capture rate of any model (84.0%), proving the power of autonomous reinforcement learning.
